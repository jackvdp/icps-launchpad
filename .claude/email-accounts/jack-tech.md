# Private consultancy mail

Jack's own address, used for the NOMOS/Buzzmint consultancy he runs
independently of ICPS: approach letters to electoral commissions, the
deep-dive content pipeline, interviews, and the one-to-one introductions.

Outlook does not hold this account, so **both composes and replies go through
Apple Mail**. Apple Mail composes carry a plain-text body, so drafts here keep
their links inline as readable URLs rather than as hyperlinked text.

There is no signature configured on this account, so drafts sign off in full:

```
Kind regards,

Jack Vanderpump
Editor, NOMOS content platform
jack@vanderpump.tech
```

The inbox is called `INBOX`, in capitals, as IMAP names it. Apple Mail matches
mailbox names exactly, so `Inbox` finds nothing here.

## Triage

Everything here is addressed to Jack directly, so the CC-trap that governs the
ICPS inbox does not apply. The volume is low and the threads are his, so a
sweep here is mostly reply-or-file.

Two rules still hold. A thread whose last message is Jack's is a park, not a
chase: approaches to commissions go quiet for weeks and that is normal. And an
approach sitting in the inbox with no reply is usually a deliberate reminder to
follow up on his own schedule, so park it rather than filing it into
`Folders/Nomos – Election Contacts`; the per-election CSVs in
`projects/nomos-consultancy/` are where chase timing is actually decided.

Keep the two sides apart. This is a private consultancy, separate from the
ICPS/NOMOS partnership, and neither should appear in the other's
correspondence. Anything that belongs to ICPS gets answered from
[jack-icps](jack-icps.md), never from here.
